# My Rental Fleet

The live site is still the simple version. The phone recording from Nov 8, 2026 is the target. Build one feature at a time on this project. Do not replace working screens with a new design from scratch.

## Look

Use the light, warm color scheme from that recording. Cream background, off-white cards, black text, orange for the main action, green for available and OK, a dark pill for the selected tab. It should feel welcoming, not like the current dark screens.

## Checkout and check-in

A customer may be waiting, so the screen has to stay fast. Photos stay sharp as thumbnails, open large, and pinch to zoom. Each walk-around spot can hold a few angles. The rental card shows the checkout photo and the return photo side by side.

If there is no signal, the inspection still saves on the phone, including its photos and video. It uploads when service comes back. The card is not lost.

## Saving a card

Saving a rental card onto the phone or computer includes the written record and all media attached to that rental.

## Media storage

Keep media in regular storage for 60 days, then move it to the cheaper tier. Plan to delete the media after one year. Keep the written rental record.
